Guest Information

A few details to help you join us.

When should I arrive?

Visitation and viewing begin at VIEWING CST on DATE. The funeral service begins at SERVICE CST. Please allow time to arrive and be seated before the service.

What time zone should I use?

All service times are Central Standard Time in Gary, Indiana. For Indianapolis-area travelers, Gary is one hour behind Indianapolis: VIEWING CST is VIEWING_EASTERN EST, and SERVICE CST is SERVICE_EASTERN EST.

What should I wear?

Respectful funeral attire is welcome. The immediate family will wear black. Please avoid fuchsia dresses or predominantly fuchsia outfits, reserving that color as Lynda’s special tribute.
